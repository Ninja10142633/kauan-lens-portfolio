# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Potential photography clients (events, portraits, commercial) looking for a premium, professional photographer in Luís Eduardo Magalhães, Bahia, and art directors/visitors seeking to view Kauan's autoral work.

## Product Purpose
A portfolio that showcases Kauan's (lens.by.zy) high-quality real-world event and autoral photography. It aims to generate bookings and present his work in a premium, elegant, and cinematic layout.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, Minimalist, Premium. Evokes confidence, artistic quality, and exclusive curation.

## Anti-references
Generic warm-beige SaaS templates, overused blue-purple gradient text, standard flat card grids with identical icon + title + paragraph styling, and typical "AI-generated looking" templates.

## Design Principles
- **Cinematic Framing:** Leverage photography-inspired elements (such as viewfinder brackets and monospace metadata) to emphasize the camera lens.
- **Editorial Restraint:** Use whitespace, crisp typography, and subtle micro-interactions to create a gallery-like, premium atmosphere.
- **Authentic Showcasing:** Focus on the real photographs and layout rather than generic illustrations or excessive decorative shapes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text (DM Sans on a near-black #080808 background).
- WCAG Level AA compatibility.
- Alternative styles for reduced-motion users (e.g. crossfades instead of movement-based animation).
